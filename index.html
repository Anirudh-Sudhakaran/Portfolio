Context

Situation & Challenge

I led a transformation initiative to transition an Excel-based script return process used by external partners in Jamaica to a structured digital approach, replacing a highly manual, inconsistent, and access-risky process. The challenge was balancing competing priorities: operational teams needed efficiency, Data Protection and Information Security focused on control, and senior stakeholders were concerned about limited timelines and complexity for external staff with high turnover.

Action Taken

I ran targeted stakeholder meetings to align the groups and proposed a structured solution using a live Microsoft SharePoint list with a customized edit form. This design allowed external users to update only required fields while protecting the underlying data and access controls.

To reduce uncertainty, I created wireframes and an initial prototype. I then facilitated solution design sessions with senior stakeholders, walking them through the benefits, controls, and technical constraints. Rather than pushing the design, I focused on addressing their concerns with evidence, simplicity, and clear explanation, followed by live support, SOPs, and training.

Outcome & Impact

The outcome was a much more structured and controlled process with greater visibility, reduced ambiguity, and a stronger foundation for automation. More importantly, it proved my ability to influence senior stakeholders, resolve competing operational and compliance priorities, and progress complex change initiatives.

Context

My involvement in delivering a significant or complex change initiative within a business area. Focusing on the nature of the change, what responsibilities I hold and what impact my contribution have on the final outcome.

Situation & Challenge

I supported the design and implementation of a new administrative charges process in City & Guilds Operations to recover costs from customer behaviors driving unnecessary workload. A key challenge was weak legacy system data quality. Initial fully automated charging runs led to invoicing errors and required customer refunds, indicating that a purely automated mechanism was unsafe.

Action Taken

I shaped the proposal alongside the Senior Leadership Team and presented it to the Operations Board. To resolve the data issues, I built a reliable validation source by pulling dimensional warehouse tables and merging manual vendor scanning data.

I designed a controlled interim solution using a SharePoint list supported by a three-stage quality assurance process. This gave operational teams a structured mechanism to validate data accuracy before invoicing files were sent to Finance. I also managed process documentation and operational readiness.

Outcome & Impact

The process moved the business from an ad-hoc approach to a highly controlled, repeatable, and auditable operation. Crucially, the system successfully recovered close to £200,000 in invoiced charges over the summer period, proving both operational governance and significant commercial value.
